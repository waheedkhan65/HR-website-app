## ABOUT US

### Built by HR practitioners who understand what small and medium businesses actually need

Neotanix is an HR consultancy and outsourced HR services company based in Islamabad, Pakistan, helping small and medium-sized businesses build HR departments that are structured, compliant, and audit-ready — without the cost of hiring a full internal team.

**Why we exist**

Most SMEs grow fast on the product and sales side, and HR gets left behind — a folder of unsigned contracts, no formal policies, no compliance trail, and no one clearly responsible for the employee lifecycle. That gap doesn't matter until it does: a labor dispute, a compliance audit, an investor due diligence process, or simply a team that's outgrown ad-hoc management.

Neotanix exists to close that gap — bringing years of hands-on HR experience to companies that need real HR infrastructure but aren't ready (or don't need) to build a large internal department.

**What we do**

We act as your outsourced HR department, or work alongside your existing team, to build and run:

- Documented, compliant HR policies and employee handbooks
- Audit-ready HR record-keeping and processes
- Payroll and benefits administration
- Performance management systems
- Recruitment, when you need to hire
- Ongoing HR advisory as your company evolves

**Our approach**

We don't sell templates. Every business has different risk exposure, culture, and growth stage — so we start by assessing where your HR function actually stands today, then build the specific structure you need, in the order that matters most (usually compliance and documentation first, then systems, then growth-stage HR like performance management and culture-building).

**Where we work**

Headquartered in Islamabad, Neotanix works with small and medium businesses across Pakistan, as well as international companies with distributed or Pakistan-based teams who need reliable, compliant HR management on the ground.

**Leadership**

Neotanix is led by HR practitioners with over a decade of hands-on experience running HR functions for technology and growth-focused organizations — not consultants working from templates.

Our leadership has personally built and led audit-ready HR infrastructure for US-based and Pakistan-based tech companies, representing HR through **SOC 2, ISO 27001, HITRUST, ISMS, and CMMI compliance audits with zero high-risk findings**. That experience spans full-cycle HR operations — workforce planning, talent acquisition, performance management, compensation, payroll governance, and HR analytics — across multi-country teams and HRIS platforms including Workday, BambooHR, Zoho People, and Odoo.

This is the same rigor we bring to every Neotanix client: HR systems built to be audited, not just to look good on paper.

## SERVICES

### HR infrastructure for small and medium businesses — built to hold up under real scrutiny

**1. Outsourced HR Department**
We become your HR function — end to end. From day-to-day employee queries to policy enforcement, documentation, and compliance, we run the department so you don't have to build one internally.

**2. HR Compliance & Audit Readiness**
We assess your current HR practices against labor law and audit standards, then close the gaps — contracts, records, policies, and processes — so you're ready for a labor inspection, financial audit, or investor due diligence at any time.

**3. Certification & Security Compliance Audits (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HITRUST, ISMS, CMMI)**
If your company needs to pass a SOC 2, ISO 27001, HITRUST, ISMS, or CMMI audit to close enterprise deals or satisfy investors, we prepare the HR side of that audit — policies, access controls, employee documentation, background verification, and governance records — led by practitioners who have represented HR through these exact audits with zero high-risk findings.

**4. HRIS & HR Systems Implementation**
Choosing and setting up the right HR system shouldn't be guesswork. We help you select, configure, and roll out HRIS/HRMS platforms (Workday, BambooHR, Zoho People, Odoo, and others) suited to your size and budget, with clean data migration and manager/employee training included.

**5. Policy Design & Employee Handbooks**
Clear, compliant, and actually usable policies covering leave, conduct, disciplinary process, termination, and everything in between — documented properly and communicated to your team.

**6. Payroll & Benefits Administration**
Accurate, on-time payroll processing and benefits management, structured to comply with local tax and labor requirements.

**7. Performance Management Systems**
Goal-setting frameworks, review cycles, and performance documentation that give you a defensible, structured basis for promotions, raises, and — when necessary — terminations.

**8. HR Consultancy & Advisory**
Ad-hoc or retained advisory for specific HR challenges — restructuring, disciplinary cases, compensation benchmarking, or building out HR strategy as you scale.

**9. Technical & Non-Technical Recruitment**
When you do need to hire, we support sourcing and screening for both technical and general roles — sourced and vetted with the same rigor we bring to HR compliance work.

**10. Staff Augmentation & Remote Engineering Teams**
Need to extend your team with specialized engineering talent — without the overhead of a full local hire? We provide vetted Software Engineers, GIS Engineers, and Structural Engineers on a remote staff augmentation basis, working in your time zone with real-time overlap for meetings, standups, and collaboration. Ideal for international companies looking to scale technical or engineering capacity through Pakistan-based talent, backed by our full HR infrastructure for contracting, compliance, and ongoing people management.

**How we work**

1. **HR Assessment** — We review your current HR practices, documentation, and compliance exposure.
2. **Gap Report** — You get a clear picture of what's missing and what's at risk, prioritized by urgency.
3. **Build & Implement** — We design and roll out the policies, systems, and processes you need.
4. **Run or Hand Off** — We either continue running your HR function on an ongoing basis, or fully hand off a documented, self-sufficient HR system to your team.
5. **Ongoing Support** — Retained advisory as regulations, headcount, or business needs change.
