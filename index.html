Why we exist

Neotanix is an HR consultancy and outsourced HR services company based in Islamabad, Pakistan, helping small and medium-sized businesses build HR departments that are structured, compliant, and audit-ready — without the cost of hiring a full internal team.

Most SMEs grow fast on the product and sales side, and HR gets left behind — a folder of unsigned contracts, no formal policies, no compliance trail, and no one clearly responsible for the employee lifecycle. That gap doesn't matter until it does: a labor dispute, a compliance audit, an investor due diligence process, or simply a team that's outgrown ad-hoc management.

Neotanix exists to close that gap — bringing years of hands-on HR experience to companies that need real HR infrastructure but aren't ready (or don't need) to build a large internal department.

What we do

  • Technical and Non Technical Recruitement
  • Documented, compliant HR policies and employee handbooks
  • Audit-ready HR record-keeping and processes
  • Payroll and benefits administration
  • Performance management systems
  • Recruitment, when you need to hire
  • Ongoing HR advisory as your company evolves

Our approach

We don't sell templates. Every business has different risk exposure, culture, and growth stage — so we start by assessing where your HR function actually stands today, then build the specific structure you need, in the order that matters most (usually compliance and documentation first, then systems, then growth-stage HR like performance management and culture-building).

Where we work

Headquartered in Islamabad, Neotanix works with small and medium businesses across Pakistan, as well as international companies with distributed or Pakistan-based teams who need reliable, compliant HR management on the ground.

Leadership

Neotanix is led by HR practitioners with over a decades of hands-on experience running HR functions for technology and growth-focused organizations — not consultants working from templates.

Our leadership has personally built and led audit-ready HR infrastructure for US-based and Pakistan-based tech companies, representing HR through SOC 2, ISO 27001, HITRUST, ISMS, and CMMI compliance audits with zero high-risk findings. That experience spans full-cycle HR operations — workforce planning, talent acquisition, performance management, compensation, payroll governance, and HR analytics — across multi-country teams and HRIS platforms including Workday, BambooHR, Zoho People, and Odoo.

This is the same rigor we bring to every Neotanix client: HR systems built to be audited, not just to look good on paper.
